常见问题

怎么用？

  • 写日记：在首页点鸟巢。一天可以写很多篇，想到什么写什么。
  • 孵今天的蛋：点底部的日历图标，在鸟巢那儿按住母鸡。这一天的日记会收成一颗蛋，蛋的样子跟这一天的心情有关。
  • 看过去的日子：点底部的日历图标。周条可以左右滑，往下拉能展开成月历。
  • 和母鸡聊天：点底部右边的圆形气泡。

母鸡没接我写的内容，只说「收好啦」？

母鸡读日记需要联网。没网或网络很慢时，日记会先存好，一个字都不会少，只是这一篇母鸡没法回你了。

如果一直都是这样：中国大陆版的 iPhone 第一次打开时会问「是否允许使用数据」，选了「不允许」母鸡就连不上网。到 iPhone 的「设置 → 母鸡日记 → 无线数据」里改成「无线局域网与蜂窝网络」就好。

之前那几天的蛋没孵出来？

下次有网时打开 App，会自动补上。今天的蛋要你自己在日历页按住母鸡孵。

母鸡什么时候会主动跟我说话？

不是每天都有，她有话想对你说的时候才会开口。

我的日记存在哪里？谁能看到？

存在你自己的手机上，没有账号，我的服务器上也没有你的日记。用到 AI 功能时，相关内容会发给 DeepSeek 处理，详见隐私政策。

怎么撤回 AI 授权？

首页右上角的齿轮 → 撤回 AI 授权。撤回之后不再发送任何内容，日记都还在，重新同意就能接着用。

怎么删除日记？

在日历页长按日记卡片，点角上出现的 × 就能删除。删除 App 会清除手机上的全部数据。

换手机时，日记能带过去吗？

没有账号和云同步，但用 iCloud 云备份恢复新手机、或用「快速开始」迁移时，日记会一起过去。直接删除 App 的话，日记就找不回来了。

FAQ

How do I use it?

  • Write an entry: tap the nest on the home screen. Write as many as you like each day.
  • Hatch today's egg: tap the calendar icon at the bottom, then press and hold the hen at the nest. The day's entries become one egg, whose look reflects how the day felt.
  • Look back: tap the calendar icon at the bottom. Swipe the week strip sideways, or pull it down to open the month view.
  • Chat with the hen: tap the round speech-bubble button at the bottom right.

The hen didn't respond to what I wrote — she only said she'd kept it safe?

Reading your entry needs an internet connection. When you're offline or the network is very slow, your entry is still saved in full — the hen just can't reply to that one.

If it happens every time: on iPhones sold in mainland China, the first launch asks whether the app may use data. If “Don't Allow” was chosen, the hen can't connect. Go to iPhone Settings → 母鸡日记 → Wireless Data and choose “WLAN & Cellular Data”.

Eggs from earlier days haven't hatched?

They'll hatch automatically the next time you open the app online. Today's egg hatches when you press and hold the hen on the calendar page.

When does the hen speak up on her own?

Not every day — only when she has something she wants to say to you.

Where is my diary stored? Who can see it?

On your own iPhone. There are no accounts, and your diary is not on my servers. When you use an AI feature, the relevant content is sent to DeepSeek. See the Privacy Policy for details.

How do I withdraw AI consent?

Tap the gear at the top right of the home screen → Withdraw AI consent (撤回 AI 授权). After that nothing is sent. Your entries stay on the device; agree again at any time to keep using it.

How do I delete an entry?

On the calendar page, press and hold a diary card, then tap the × on its corner. Deleting the app removes all data on the device.

Can I move my diary to a new iPhone?

There are no accounts or cloud sync, but your diary moves along when you restore from an iCloud backup or use Quick Start. If you delete the app, your diary cannot be recovered.
